Delete my Switch data

This page explains how to delete data held by Switch (switchcardgame.co.uk). Switch works without an account, so most of your data is only on your phone and you can delete it yourself.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Switch and go to Me, then Privacy.
  2. Choose Delete data on this device to remove your players, scores, history and settings from this phone.
  3. If you signed in with Google, choose Delete my cloud copy to delete your cloud backup and sign out.

Ask us to delete shared copies

Deleting data in the app does not remove a night or club you shared, because other people may be using it. To have a shared copy deleted, email martinwhite228@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Switch data" and tell us:

  • the 6-letter night code or 8-character club code, and/or
  • the Google account email you used to sign in, if you want a cloud backup deleted and cannot use the in-app button.

We will delete the copy and reply to confirm, within one month. We may ask you to prove you hold the code or account. Anyone else who has the code will lose access to the deleted night or club.

What we cannot delete

Anonymous usage totals contain no personal information, so they cannot be traced back to you and are not deleted. Our host (Vercel) keeps standard server logs briefly for security.
