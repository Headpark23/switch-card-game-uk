Switch privacy

Short version: Switch keeps everything on your phone. We do not sell data and we do not run ads or trackers.

What is stored, and where

  • Player names, scores, night history, puzzle progress and settings are saved in your browser or app storage on this device only.
  • The app works offline. Nothing needs to be sent anywhere to play, learn or keep score.
  • Share pictures are made on your phone. You choose where they go.

Optional sign-in and backup

If you choose Google sign-in, Google tells us your name and email so we can attach an optional cloud backup of your club data to you. The backup contains the names and results you entered. You can stay signed out and nothing is uploaded.

Your choices

  • You can export or delete your night history in the app at any time.
  • Clearing the app's site data removes everything stored on the device.
  • To remove a cloud backup, contact us using the address below.

Children

Switch is a family card game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
