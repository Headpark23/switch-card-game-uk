Switch privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026.

Short version: Switch keeps your club on your phone. Playing, learning and keeping score work offline and send nothing anywhere. A few optional features (sharing a night, sharing a club, Google sign-in backup) send the names and scores you choose to share to our server. We also add up anonymous usage totals, which you can turn off. We do not sell data, show ads or use advertising IDs.

What is stored on your phone

  • Player names, scores, night history, computer-game results, puzzle progress and settings are saved in this device's app storage.
  • Share pictures are made on your phone. You choose where they go.

Everything is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We use the information only to make these features work and to understand, in totals, how the app is used. Google may see that you used its sign-in button.

How long we keep it

  • Usage counts: kept as daily totals. They contain no personal information.
  • Cloud backup: until you delete it. Use "Delete my cloud copy" in Me › Privacy, or ask us.
  • Shared night: it can no longer be opened after two weeks. We remove the stored copy when you ask.
  • Shared club: kept until you ask us to delete it. Leaving a club in the app only stops that phone syncing; it does not delete the shared copy.
  • Feedback emails: until you ask us to delete them.
  • Our host (Vercel) keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, briefly for security.

Your choices and how to delete your data

  • In the app (Me › Privacy) you can download your data, delete everything stored on the device, delete your cloud backup, and turn off usage counts.
  • Deleting data on a device does not delete copies you shared (a night, a club or a backup). To have those removed, see how to ask us to delete your data.
  • Clearing the app's site or app data removes everything stored on the device.

Who is responsible

M. White is the data controller. Contact martinwhite228@gmail.com. Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export, restrict or erase your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Other companies involved

Vercel hosts the app and stores shared nights, clubs, backups and usage totals. Google provides sign-in. Data may be processed in the USA under their standard safeguards. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Children

Switch is a family card game and works without an account. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not sign in, or share a night or club with children's names, without a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top will change and the in-app Privacy screen will be updated to match.
