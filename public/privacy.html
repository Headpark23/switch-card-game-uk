Switch privacy

Short version: Switch keeps your club on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, a private backup of your club is also kept so it can follow you to another phone. We do not sell data and we do not run ads or trackers.

What is stored, and where

  • Player names, scores, night history, puzzle progress and settings are saved in your browser or app storage on this device. If you sign in, a copy is also backed up to your account (see below).
  • The app works offline. Nothing needs to be sent anywhere to play, learn or keep score.
  • Share pictures are made on your phone. You choose where they go.

Optional sign-in and backup

If you choose Google sign-in, Google tells us your name, email and profile picture so we can attach a cloud backup of your club to you. The backup is stored privately, readable only by your account, and contains the names and results you entered. We use it only to restore your club on your devices. You can stay signed out and nothing is uploaded.

Your choices

  • You can export or delete your night history in the app at any time.
  • Clearing the app's site data removes everything stored on the device.
  • To remove a cloud backup, email martinwhite228@gmail.com.

Who is responsible

M. White is the data controller. Contact martinwhite228@gmail.com. Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export, restrict or erase your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Shared nights, visit counter and feedback

If you share a night, its title, first names and scores are stored under a six-letter code and deleted after two weeks. The app counts visits with a single number; no cookie, device ID or IP address is saved by Switch. Our host (Vercel) keeps standard server logs briefly for security. The feedback buttons open your own email app, and we only receive what you send.

Data may be processed by Vercel and Google, including in the USA, under their standard safeguards. Please do not sign in if you are under 13 without a parent or guardian.

Children

Switch is a family card game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
