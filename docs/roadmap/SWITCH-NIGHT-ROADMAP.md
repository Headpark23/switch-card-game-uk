# Switch Night roadmap

Switch remains a focused solo app. The next improvements should deepen the Switch experience before any multi-game product is started.

## Phase 1 — local history

- Save completed nights as dated, named events.
- Show winners, final tables, knockout paths, and cup brackets.
- Add export and delete controls, with clear confirmation before removal.
- Keep the existing scorekeeper key compatible and migrate additively.

## Phase 2 — family groups

- Make Google sign-in optional; the app must continue to work without an account.
- Identify people by the verified Google `sub`, never by an editable display name or email alone.
- Let a signed-in person create or join a private family group through an expiring invite.
- Provide owner/member roles, leaving/removal controls, and an audit trail for shared-score changes.
- Define conflict handling before sync: local unsent changes must not be silently overwritten.

## Phase 3 — friend sharing

- Share a read-only night summary first, with a revocable link and no account required to view.
- Add collaborative scoring only after permissions, offline recovery, and conflict resolution are proven.
- Never publish names or results by default, and never manufacture social activity.

## Phase 4 — deeper Switch Night

- Season summaries, rivalries, streaks, and family trophies derived only from genuine recorded games.
- Optional rematch setups and reusable player groups.
- Accessible print/export views for league tables and cup brackets.
- Privacy controls for export, account deletion, group deletion, and retention.

## Separate future product

Only after Switch is stable should its rules, tutorial, scoring, competition, history, and sharing patterns become a blueprint for a separate games companion covering cards, dominoes, Yahtzee-style dice games, and board games. That product should be a new app rather than expanding Switch until it loses its identity.
