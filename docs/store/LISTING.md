**Full description**

Switch is the companion for the card game you play with real cards.

- Learn the rules in five steps, then practise against the computer. A coach explains every move: what was played, the rule, why it matters and what you could have played.
- Test yourself with rules puzzles. Build a streak and earn badges from Rookie to Rules master.
- Settle any argument in seconds with searchable answers and quick-pick topics.
- Keep score the easy way: Quick night is one tap per round. Or run Domino, Darts, Knockout, Group leagues and Switch Cup nights.
- See history, stats, leaderboards and share a picture of the night.
- Works offline. Your data stays on your phone.
