**Full description**

Switch is the classic British card game, played with an ordinary deck: match the suit or number, hit your friends with pick-up cards, change the suit with an ace, and be the first to empty your hand. Also known as Last Card, Black Jack or Two Four Jack, it's a grown-up cousin of Crazy Eights, with real tactics and real arguments.

Your game-night club, with four ways to play:

**Play with friends and family online**
- Start a private table and share a code. Friends join from their own phones, and computers fill any empty seats.
- No accounts and no chat with strangers: just your table, and 18 fun reactions to send.
- If someone wanders off, a countdown warns them and the computer plays their move.

**Play against the computer**
- Take on 1 to 7 computer players (up to 8 at the table, with two decks for big games).
- Easy, Medium or Hard. Every computer has its own personality and favourite tactics.
- Single game, Knock Out, Group Play leagues with play-offs, Switch Cup, and Darts or Dominoes scoring.
- Every game counts towards your record, streaks, trophies and badges.

**Keep score at the real table**
- Quick night is one tap per round. Or run Domino, Darts, Knock Out, Group Play and Switch Cup nights.
- Fix any mistake: undo rounds, change Cup results, settle ties with a deciding game.
- Save the night in one tap and share a picture of the results.

**Clubs and competitions**
- Share your club with an 8-character code or link. Players, nights, leagues and seasons sync between everyone's phones automatically.
- Friends who join a night get every result in their own history, even if their app was closed.
- Seasons keep the long-running table across nights, with leaderboards and stats.

**Learn the rules and get better**
- Learn the rules in five steps, then play a guided practice game. A coach explains every move: what was played, the rule, why it matters and what you could have played. Hints are there when you want them.
- Test yourself with rules puzzles and settle any argument in seconds with searchable answers.
- Earn badges from Rookie to Rules master, and see every game you've ever played in Me > History.

Works offline for playing the computer, learning and scoring. Online tables, shared nights and clubs, and Google backup only send data when you choose to use them. No ads and no purchases.
